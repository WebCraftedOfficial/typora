    Improve your typing speed with a free online typing test

    Typora is a free online typing test and typing speed practice tool that helps you understand how quickly and accurately you type. Take a typing speed test to check your words per minute (WPM), choose a short or longer test, practise with everyday words or more challenging passages, and review your results when the timer ends. Use this fast typing practice online to build keyboard confidence and track your progress over time.

    English and Hindi typing practice

    Typora supports typing practice in English and Hindi. Choose your language in the test controls, select a duration and difficulty, and type the passage shown on screen. English speed is shown in words per minute (WPM). Hindi speed is shown in characters per minute (CPM), because Hindi uses joined characters and vowel marks that do not map neatly to English word counting.

    हिंदी टाइपिंग टेस्ट के लिए भाषा में “हिन्दी” चुनें। फिर समय और कठिनाई का स्तर चुनकर स्क्रीन पर दिया गया पाठ टाइप करें। हिंदी अभ्यास में गति CPM (प्रति मिनट अक्षर) में दिखाई जाती है। सही टाइपिंग के लिए अपने डिवाइस पर हिंदी इनपुट, इनस्क्रिप्ट या फोनेटिक कीबोर्ड चालू करें।

    What people look for in a typing practice test

    A useful typing test practice session should do more than show a speed number. It should let you practise at different durations, choose a difficulty level, measure accuracy, and review mistakes. Typora brings these tools together in one free browser-based typing practice test for English and Hindi.

    How to use this typing test

    • Choose 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds for your test.
    • Select Words or Quotes, then choose Easy, Medium or Advanced.
    • Press Start typing and enter the text shown on the screen.
    • Review your WPM or CPM, accuracy, correct characters and consistency estimate.
    • Repeat the test later and compare your results as you practise.

    Fast typing practice for beginners and regular learners

    If you are new to typing, begin with the easy level and focus on accurate keystrokes before trying to increase your speed. For regular practice, try a 30-second or 60-second typing test, then repeat it to compare your WPM and accuracy. Typora also lets you practise with quotes or your own text, making it a simple typing practice test for different skill levels.

    What do WPM, accuracy and consistency mean?

    WPM (words per minute) is a standard way to describe typing speed. Typora uses the common five-characters-per-word convention and calculates speed from correctly typed characters over the elapsed time. Accuracy shows the percentage of characters you entered that match the displayed text. Consistency is an estimate of how steady your recent speed has been during the test; it is not a measure of typing skill or a universal score.

    A useful practice tip: aim for comfortable, accurate typing first. Speed often improves as you become more familiar with the keyboard and build a steady rhythm.

    Practise with text that matters to you

    Use the Custom text option to practise with your own paragraph, study notes, a work passage or any text you want to type. Typora runs the test in your browser, so the text entered into the typing area is processed on your device rather than uploaded to a Typora server.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I take a typing speed test?

    Choose a duration and difficulty, select Words or Quotes, and press Start typing. Type the displayed text as accurately as possible. Your speed and accuracy update while you practise.

    What does WPM mean in a typing test?

    WPM means words per minute. Typora treats every five correctly typed characters as one standard word and divides by the elapsed time in minutes.

    How is typing accuracy calculated?

    Accuracy is the percentage of characters you typed that match the displayed text at the same position. Correct characters are divided by all characters entered.

    Can I take a Hindi typing test on Typora?

    Yes. Choose हिन्दी from the Language control to practise Hindi passages and quotes. Hindi speed is measured in characters per minute (CPM), and you should enable a Hindi input method on your device before typing.

    Can I practise with my own text?

    Yes. Select Custom text and paste a passage you want to practise. You can use your own study notes or any text you have permission to use.

    Is Typora free to use?

    Yes. Typora's typing test is free to use in a modern web browser.

    Is my typing text uploaded?

    The typing test runs in your browser. The text you enter in the test is processed locally by the page and is not sent to a Typora server.
